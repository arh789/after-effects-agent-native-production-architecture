# Publishing Rules

The delivery folder should make publication possible without reopening the
editing history. Prepare:

- final clean transcript;
- verified caption file;
- YouTube title, description, chapters, links, and pinned comment;
- TikTok caption, cover text, hashtags, and series order;
- companion-article handoff with video URL, thesis, tags, and related pages;
- clearly named platform and archival exports.

Chapter timestamps must derive from the confirmed timeline, not from estimates.
Titles and thumbnail text should create complementary tension rather than repeat
one another verbatim.
